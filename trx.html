Is this for you?

TRX is a good fit if any of these sound familiar.

  • You want real strength work that's kind to your joints.
  • Once a week fits alongside the other things you do.
  • You like being able to make it easier or harder on the spot.

Inside a session

You train with straps anchored above you, working against your own bodyweight and gravity. Change your angle and the same exercise gets easier or harder, so everyone in the group gets the right challenge at the same time. Nearly every move works your core.

Your sessions work across every program, so TRX pairs well with TribeLIFE or TribeFIT.

Is this for you?

TribeLIFE is a good fit if any of these sound familiar.

  • You want to get stronger without jumping or high-impact moves.
  • Staying mobile and capable as the years go on matters to you.
  • You like structure, and a coach who adjusts things for you.

Inside a session

Every session balances full-body aerobic movement, functional exercises that move in every direction, and core and mobility work, all easy on your joints. The program runs for eight weeks and progresses as you do, so each session builds on the last.

Your sessions also work for TRX, so you can add a strength-and-balance day whenever you like.

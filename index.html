Why group training works

Most people don't need more workout ideas. They need a plan, someone watching their form, and a reason to show up on a Tuesday night.

A coach who's watching you

With eight people at most, your coach corrects your form, modifies moves when you need it, and knows what you're working toward.

A plan, not just a class

Each program runs for four or eight weeks and progresses as you do, so every session builds on the last.

People who notice

The same small group, week after week. Accountability is easier when people expect to see you.

Room to explore

Your monthly sessions work for any group training program, so you can mix formats or move up when you're ready.

How is this different from group fitness classes?

Group fitness and group training are both great ways to stay active. They just work differently. Group fitness classes are included with fitness memberships and are an easy way to keep moving. Group training adds a smaller group, a coach who adjusts for you, and a plan that builds over several weeks. Pick the one that suits your goals, or enjoy both.

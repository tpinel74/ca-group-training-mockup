Is this for you?

Move to Live More is a good fit if any of these sound familiar.

  • You haven't exercised in a while and a busy gym floor feels like a lot.
  • Life got in the way and you want to rebuild a routine.
  • You'd like to feel at home in a fitness club before going it alone.

Inside a session

Each 45-minute session mixes a little health education with aerobic, strength and core work, all pitched at your level. Expect simple, practical exercises, some games and challenges, and a coach who makes sure every move works for your body. You'll try different equipment around the club, so by week four the place feels like yours.

When the four weeks are up, your coach will help you choose what's next, whether that's another group training program or carrying on with confidence on your own.
